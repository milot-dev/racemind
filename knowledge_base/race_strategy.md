# Race Strategy

Race strategy in MotoGP involves balancing speed, tire management, risk, and track position.

## Starting Grid
A better grid position can reduce traffic and help a rider control the opening laps.

## Tire Management
Riders must manage tire grip across the race. Pushing too hard early can reduce performance later.

## Overtaking
Overtaking requires timing, braking confidence, corner positioning, and sometimes slipstream.

## Risk Management
A rider may choose a safer strategy to secure points instead of risking a crash while fighting for a higher position.

## Comeback Race
A comeback race happens when a rider starts from a lower grid position but finishes much higher through overtakes, pace, and consistency.
