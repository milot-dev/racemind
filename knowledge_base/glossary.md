# MotoGP Glossary

## DNF
Did Not Finish. The rider started the race but did not complete it.

## DNS
Did Not Start. The rider did not start the session or race.

## Pole Position
The first starting position on the grid, earned through qualifying.

## Podium
A top-three finish.

## Highside
A crash where the rear tire loses and then suddenly regains grip, throwing the rider over the bike.

## Lowside
A crash where the bike slides out from underneath the rider, usually due to loss of grip.

## Apex
The inside point of a corner where the rider aims to pass closest before accelerating out.
