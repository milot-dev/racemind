# MotoGP Basics

MotoGP is the highest class of Grand Prix motorcycle road racing. Riders compete on prototype racing motorcycles designed for maximum performance.

A MotoGP weekend usually includes practice sessions, qualifying, sprint races, and the main Grand Prix race.

## Race
The main race awards the largest number of points and usually has the greatest impact on the championship.

## Sprint
Sprint races are shorter races held during the weekend. They reward aggressive racing and can influence championship momentum.

## Qualifying
Qualifying determines the starting grid. A strong qualifying result helps a rider avoid traffic and reduce early-race risk.

## Championship Points
Riders earn points based on finishing position. More points usually indicate stronger performance across the season.
