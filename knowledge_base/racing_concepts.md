# Racing Concepts

## Race Pace
Race pace is the speed or lap time a rider can maintain consistently during a race. It is different from qualifying pace, which is usually focused on producing one very fast lap.

## Qualifying Pace
Qualifying pace is the ability to produce a fast lap over a short run. A rider can have excellent qualifying pace but weaker race pace if they cannot maintain tire performance or consistency.

## Tire Degradation
Tire degradation is the loss of tire grip and performance during a race. In motorcycle racing, tire degradation affects braking stability, corner speed, acceleration, and rider confidence.

## Slipstream
Slipstream occurs when one rider follows another closely and benefits from reduced aerodynamic drag. This can increase straight-line speed and help overtaking.

## Corner Entry
Corner entry refers to the braking and turning phase as a rider enters a corner.

## Corner Exit
Corner exit refers to how well a rider accelerates out of a corner. A strong corner exit can improve speed on the following straight.

## Consistency
Consistency means maintaining competitive lap times across a race distance. A rider with strong consistency may beat a rider who is faster for only a few laps.
